Task Module – Interview SummarySource:Interview Summary.doc1. Executive SummaryThis document captures Task Module insights derived solely from the Interview Summary, focusing on lifecycle, actions, rationale capture, layout preferences, and cross-business visibility.2. Key Themes & ImplicationsThemeInsight / RequirementUser Need / Pain PointImplication for UXTask lifecycle & remindersTasks must be explicitly Completed or Rejected; otherwise they keep reappearing as Overdue. Capture the reason when rejecting.Users are nagged by persistent overdue items; need a conclusive way to resolve or dismiss with context.Provide clear Complete/Reject actions; require reason on Reject; consider Snooze/Dismiss with reason; display overdue badges and filters.Rejection rationale (scenarios)Include structured scenario questions to understand why a task is rejected.Lack of structured reasons makes it hard to find patterns and improve processes.Design a guided reason picker with optional notes and analytics tagging.Layout orientationGrid views cause confusion about reading order; preference for single-page / linear view.Users feel disoriented in multi-panel grids and are unsure of progression.Default to linear list or stepper with explicit numbering; if a grid is used, add orientation cues and offer a toggle to single-page view.Multi-business area visibilityStakeholders from Motor asked for their screens in a multi-area app.Each business area needs assurance that their workflows are represented.Provide a business-area switcher and label area-specific screens; include area-specific demos in reviews.3. Actionable Design Requirements·        Add prominent Complete and Reject buttons on task cards; open a required-reason dialog on Reject.·        Introduce a structured reason taxonomy (scenario picker) with free-text notes; feed analytics.·        Use a default linear list with collapsed cards; expand for details and actions.·        Highlight Overdue state clearly; add filters for Overdue and Rejected-with-reason.·        Provide a business-area selector (e.g., tabs/segmented) and area labels on task views.4. Assumptions & Out of Scope (for this version)This version excludes MOM-derived items such as SLA thresholds, escalation hierarchy, manager/admin dashboards, task creation by web users, and audit configuration. Those are covered in the Combined Requirements document.5.
