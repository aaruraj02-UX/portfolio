UX Strategy — ANPS Response Generation

📋 DocProcess Stage 3 & 5📅 10 June 2026🔖 v1.0

1. Vision

Agents review and send, not assemble. AI handles letter composition; humans ensure quality and handle exceptions — with full traceability from challenge received to letter dispatched.

2. UX Principles

AI Transparency

Every AI decision visible. Confidence scores shown. Agent always knows why AI flagged a case.

Diff-First Review

QA sees only what changed. Added/deleted passages highlighted. No need to read full letter.

Exception-Led Workflow

Green cases auto-queue for send. Amber/Red surface at the top for agent attention.

Audit Everything

Every action — AI validation, agent edit, QA decision, send event — timestamped and traceable.

Fast Knowledge Updates

Business can update source content and publish within 24h without developer involvement.

WCAG 2.2 AA

All interfaces accessible. High-contrast diff views. Screen reader support for letter review.
