`n`n`n`n`nExecutive Summary`n
`nThis Business Requirements Document (BRD) defines the combined capability of customer-facing Landing Pages and the internal Customer App Landing Page Settings module, enabling a fully controlled, secure, and business-managed digital experience.`n
`nThe customer-facing Landing Pages provide a structured, end-to-end self-service journey for customers to securely access their case information, understand their enforcement stage, and complete key actions such as making payments or seeking support. These pages are personalised post-authentication and are designed to drive clarity, reduce confusion, and increase successful digital engagement and payment conversion while maintaining compliance and accessibility standards.`n
`nComplementing this experience, the Customer App Landing Page Settings module enables authorised business users to manage and control the content displayed on these Landing Pages without requiring technical intervention. Through this capability, users can access structured content areas aligned to Landing Page sections (such as Payment, Enforcement, and Support), edit content in a format that reflects the end-user view, apply formatting, insert variables, and manage hyperlinks, while preserving the overall layout and structure of the pages.`n
`nAll content changes are governed by a structured approval workflow comprising Author, Reviewer, and Publisher roles, ensuring that updates are validated for accuracy, compliance, and policy alignment before being released to production. The system enforces segregation of duties, maintains a complete audit trail, and supports traceability through logged actions and timestamps at each stage of the approval process.`n
`nThis combined solution ensures:`n
`n
`n
`nA consistent and compliant customer experience across all Landing Pages`n`n
`n
`nBusiness-led control over content and messaging`n`n
`n
`nReduced dependency on development teams for content updates`n`n
`n
`nFull auditability and governance of all changes`n`n
`n
`nAlignment between backend case data, frontend presentation, and controlled content evolution`n`n
`n
`nThe BRD provides a comprehensive definition of functional behaviour, governance rules, workflows, and system requirements necessary to deliver and manage both the customer journey and the supporting content management capability effectively.`n

`n`n`n`n`nLanding Page Overview (End-User Journey Summary)`n
`nThe Landing Pages represent a structured, secure, and personalised digital journey that enables customers to access case information, understand their enforcement status, and complete key actions such as making a payment or seeking support. This journey is initiated through a controlled entry point and progresses through a series of defined stages designed to ensure clarity, security, and compliance.`n
`n`n
`n`nEntry and Authentication`n`n
`nThe journey begins when a customer accesses the system via a generic SMS link. Upon entry, the user is presented with a Sign-In / Identity Verification page where they must provide required credentials (such as postcode and other validation inputs). Authentication is enforced with controlled attempt limits, and access to any personal or case-related information is restricted until verification is successfully completed.`n
`nIf authentication fails, the system provides controlled retry attempts and recovery options. Upon exceeding the allowed attempts, the user is restricted from further access and directed to recovery or support channels.`n
`n`n
`n`nCase Retrieval and Personalisation`n`n
`nOnce authenticated, the system retrieves the customer's case data from backend systems. This includes key attributes such as case reference, enforcement stage, outstanding balance, and applicable fees. This data drives all subsequent page behaviour, including content personalisation, timeline positioning, and action prioritisation.`n
`nPersonalisation is dynamically applied based on verified data, ensuring that all information displayed is accurate, relevant, and contextual to the customer's situation.`n
`n`n
`n`nHome / Timeline Page (Primary Landing Page)`n`n
`nThe Home / Timeline page serves as the central point of the journey and the primary decision-making interface for the customer. This page provides:`n
`n
`n
`nA visual timeline representing the customer's enforcement stage`n`n
`n
`nA summary of the case, including balance and status`n`n
`n
`nContextual messaging explaining the current position and next steps`n`n
`n
`nA clearly defined primary call-to-action (typically "Pay Now")`n`n
`n
`nThe timeline simplifies complex backend enforcement stages into a clear, structured visual representation to improve customer understanding and reduce confusion.`n
`n`n
`n`nCustomer Decision Paths`n`n
`nFrom the Home / Timeline page, the user is guided into one of the following paths:`n
`n`n1. Payment Path (Primary Path)`n`n
`nThe user selects the primary call-to-action and is directed to the Payment page, where they can:`n
`n
`n
`nReview outstanding balance and fees`n`n
`n
`nConfirm payment details`n`n
`n
`nProceed to a secure external payment gateway`n`n
`n
`nUpon successful payment, the system confirms the transaction and updates the case status accordingly.`n
`n`n
`n`n2. Enforcement Information Path`n`n
`nUsers may choose to view additional information about their enforcement stage. This provides:`n
`n
`n
`nStage-specific explanations`n`n
`n
`nConsequences of non-payment`n`n
`n
`nClear description of what happens next`n`n
`n
`nThis path is designed to improve understanding and encourage informed decision-making without causing confusion or escalation.`n
`n`n
`n`n3. Support Path`n`n
`nUsers can access support-related content which includes:`n
`n
`n
`nFrequently asked questions`n`n
`n
`nVulnerability support options`n`n
`n
`nComplaint and assistance routes`n`n
`n
`nThis path provides reassurance and assistance while maintaining alignment with the overall journey objectives.`n
`n`n
`n`nJourney Outcomes`n`n
`nThe journey supports multiple outcomes:`n
`n
`n
`n`nSuccessful outcome:`n Payment completed and case updated`n`n
`n
`n`nReturn path:`n User navigates back to the Home page or continues the journey`n`n
`n
`n`nExit path:`n User leaves the journey or transitions to offline support channels`n`n
`n

`n`nJourney Design Principles`n`n
`nThe Landing Page journey is built on the following principles:`n
`n
`n
`n`nSecurity:`n No personal data is exposed prior to successful authentication`n`n
`n
`n`nClarity:`n Complex information is simplified through visual and structured presentation`n`n
`n
`n`nGuided Action:`n Primary actions are clearly highlighted to drive completion`n`n
`n
`n`nCompliance:`n Content and messaging follow regulatory and policy requirements`n`n
`n
`n`nContinuity:`n The journey avoids dead ends, always providing a next actionable step`n`n

`n

`n
`nEnforcement`n`n
`n
`nSupport`n`n

`nThe content editing interface includes the following capabilities:`n

`n
`n`nPanel-based organisation`n, where each panel represents a distinct content block or section`n`n
`n
`n`nPanel names mapped to Landing Page headings`n, ensuring clarity and consistency between editor and live pages`n`n
`n
`n`nPreservation of layout and structure`n, preventing unintended changes to page design`n`n
`n
`n`n

`n

`n

`n
`n

`n

`n
`nThe workflow consists of:`n
`n
`n
`n`nAuthoring`n =E2=80=93 content creation and editing`n`n

`n`nReview`n =E2=80=93 validation of content for compliance and accuracy`n`n
`n
`n`nPublishing`n =E2=80=93 final approval and release to production`n`n

`n

`n

`n

`n`n`n`n`nFunctional Requirements`n
`n`n7.1 Landing Pages (Customer-Facing)`n`n
`nThe system shall provide a set of customer-facing Landing Pages that enable a secure, personalised, and structured journey from authentication to payment completion.`n
`n
`n
`nThe system shall display a Sign-In / ID&V page as the entry point, restricting access to case data until successful verification`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall retrieve and render case data dynamically post-authentication, including enforcement stage, balance, and fees`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall display a Home / Timeline page with a visual representation of the customer's enforcement stage`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall present a primary Call-to-Action (CTA) to guide users toward payment`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall allow navigation to the Payment page, Enforcement Information pages, and Support pages from the Home page`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall display the Payment page with accurate case summary, balance, and applicable fees`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall enable redirection to a secure external payment gateway for transaction processing`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall handle payment success and failure states and provide appropriate feedback to the user`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall display Enforcement Information pages with stage-specific explanations and consequences`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall display Support pages with structured assistance options, including FAQs and escalation routes`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall ensure all pages are session-bound and accessible only after successful authentication`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall track all user interactions and key events across the Landing Page journey`n`n
`n
`n`n
`n`n7.2 Customer App =E2=80=93 Content Editor`n`n
`nThe system shall provide a Landing Page Settings module within the Customer App to enable business users to manage and control Landing Page content.`n
`n
`n
`nThe system shall display Landing Page content areas as structured cards (e.g., Payment, Enforcement, Support)`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall allow users to select and edit a specific card, navigating to a corresponding content editing screen`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall present content in a structured format that mirrors the layout and appearance of the customer-facing Landing Pages`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall organise content into panels, with each panel representing a section of the Landing Page`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall display panel names aligned with corresponding headings in the Landing Pages`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall allow users to edit content directly within panels without altering the overall page structure`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall provide formatting options to modify content presentation`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall allow insertion of variables within content`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall allow creation and configuration of hyperlinks within content`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall preserve the visual structure and layout of Landing Pages during editing`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall allow users to save content changes prior to submission`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall ensure that all content changes are captured and reflected in the audit log`n`n
`n
`n`n
`n`n7.3 Governance and Approval Workflow`n`n
`nThe system shall enforce a structured, business-led approval workflow governing all content changes.`n
`n
`n
`nThe system shall support a three-step workflow: Author =E2=86=92 Reviewer =E2=86=92 Publisher`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall allow Authors to create and submit content changes for review`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall enable integration with a ticketing system for managing change requests`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall allow assignment of Author, Reviewer, and Publisher roles per request`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall prevent a single user from performing multiple roles within the same request`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall allow Reviewers to approve or reject submitted changes`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall require Reviewers to provide feedback when rejecting changes`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall allow only approved changes to proceed to the publishing stage`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall allow Publishers to perform final review and publish content to production`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall prevent publishing without prior approval`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall capture all workflow actions including submission, approval, rejection, and publishing`n`n
`n
`nThe system shall record timestamps and user identifiers for each workflow stage`n`n

`n`n`n`n`nBusiness Rules`n
`nThe following business rules govern the behaviour of both the customer-facing Landing Pages and the Customer App Landing Page Settings module, ensuring consistency, compliance, and controlled content management.`n
`n`n
`n`nCustomer-Facing Business Rules`n`n
`n
`n
`nCustomers must not be able to access any case or personal data prior to successful authentication`n`n
`n
`nAccess to Landing Pages must be restricted to verified sessions only`n`n
`n
`nThe system must display information for one case at a time within the Landing Pages`n`n
`n
`nIn scenarios with multiple cases, a default case must be selected based on defined prioritisation logic (e.g., urgency)`n`n
`n
`nAll case-related data (balance, fees, enforcement stage) must match backend system values at all times`n`n
`n
`nEnforcement messaging must be accurate, factual, and compliant with applicable policies`n`n
`n
`nTimeline representation must reflect the mapped enforcement stage and must not be editable through the Customer App`n`n
`n
`nPayment actions must always be tied to the currently active case`n`n
`n
`nPayment attempts must not restrict or lock the user out of the overall journey`n`n
`n
`nAll navigation paths must ensure no dead-end states, providing users with clear next actions`n`n
`n
`n`n

`nPanel names within the editor must align with corresponding headings in the Landing Pages`n`n
`n
`nAll content changes must be saved before being submitted for review`n`n
`n
`nContent editing must support formatting, variable insertion, and hyperlink configuration`n`n
`n
`nAll changes made within the editor must be logged in the audit trail with user and timestamp details`n`n
