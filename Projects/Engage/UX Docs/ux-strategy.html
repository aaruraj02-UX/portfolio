1. Vision

"Every field agent knows exactly what to do next, every manager knows exactly where SLA risk is, and every action taken is traceable — without friction getting in the way."
DimensionMeaning
ClarityAgents always know their next priority task. Status, SLA, and required actions are instantly readable.
ControlManagers have real-time, actionable SLA visibility with zero reliance on manual refresh.
ConfidenceEvery lifecycle action is guided, confirmed, and auditable.

2. UX Principles

P1 · Clarity Over Completeness

Show users only what they need to act. Surface highest-urgency first. Time-to-identify < 8 seconds.

P2 · Reliability Without Noise

Data always current. Alerts are meaningful. Auto-refresh every 60s. SLA alert false-positive = 0%.

P3 · Guided Actions

Every action described, previewed, and confirmed. Users never surprised by consequences.

P4 · Role-Based Simplicity

Each role sees only relevant controls. Admin power doesn't pollute Agent simplicity.
