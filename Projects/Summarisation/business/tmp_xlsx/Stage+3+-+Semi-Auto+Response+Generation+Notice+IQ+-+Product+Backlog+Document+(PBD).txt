1. Executive Summary
The Semi-Auto Response Generation will extract the relevant information from the case details to pre-fill the relevant sections in the correspondence letter for Notice IQ clients and then once checked, will get sent out.
2. Business Objectives
* Reduce the time taken to write up the correspondence.
* Ensure that the key information is captured from the summarisation and link it to the correct and accurate correspondence templates and then add these into the letter.
* Enable seamless integration with Notice IQ portal.
* We want to see an automated complete letter and an action required letter.
* In the agent workflow we want to show a column with the title 'letter generation' that highlights the cases that are completed by the AI and cases that the AI cannot complete.
* Want the cases that need manual changes from an agent to be identified so the QA's can perform quality checks on these cases.
* Maintain complete audit logs for compliance.
3. Background & Business Context
Current State (As-Is):
* Agent must fill out the reason, sub reason and answer questions for each sub reason manually.
* Agent must downloads the letter template from the CRM.
* Agent must manually copy the relevant template paragraphs from AIRA into the downloaded letter and then upload the letter.
Future State (To-Be):
* Information from case validation, citizen evidence, previous letter history, permit/Ringo check, council rules, stage of PCN and amount outstanding is automatically captured and pre fills the answers to the questions to form a suggested letter to the customer.
* The agent checks the suggested letter and if necessary makes any changes to form the final letter.
* Once the letter is checked the agent the clicks send.
4. Scope
In Scope
* Pre fill the details from the PCN case validation (Stage 2 output) into the correspondence letter per council.
* Notice IQ Councils - Bracknell Forest, Hertfordshire, Surrey, Wokingham.
Out of Scope
* PCN challenges outside Notice IQ systems.
* Third-party CRMs.

7. Assumptions
* Council policy documents are accessible and up to date.
* Agents trained on reviewing the letter before it gets sent out.
* The current processes and progressions after a letter is completed remain the same as the processes we do today.
8. Constraints
* Limited to Notice IQ contracts.
* Dependent on council documentation accuracy.
* GDPR compliance required.
9. Risks
* Poor policy document quality may impact accuracy.
* Potential agent resistance to automation.
10. Dependencies
* Stage 3 Tool Integration -The bot depends on the stage 3 tool being available for retrieving the PCN case validation (Stage 2 output) into the correspondence letter.
* Council Policy Documents-Validation accuracy depends on each council maintaining and providing their up-to-date policy documents.
* MNPS Portal Access-Agents must access the tool only through the Notice IQ back-office portal, any downtime impacts adoption.
* User Training-Processing agents must be trained on reviewing flagged mismatches and summaries.
11. Success Criteria
* The tool can complete the majority of letters automatically (either fully or partially), with little adaption from the agents.
* Positive agent feedback on usability.
* 100% audit trail of validation outcomes.
12. Key Milestones
* Business requirements sign-off.
* Prototype delivery.
* UAT completion.
* Go-live.
* Post-implementation review.
