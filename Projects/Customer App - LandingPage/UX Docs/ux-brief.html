UX Brief — Acme Customer App Landing Page

📋 Acme Customer App — Landing Page & Settings Module 🏢 Acme Holdings 📅 21 May 2026 🔖 BRD v1.0

1. Background

Acme Holdings provides enforcement and recovery services to local authorities and central government. Customers (citizens) currently engage through inconsistent digital touchpoints and high-friction agent-assisted channels, which limits self-service adoption, slows payment conversion, and increases dispute volumes.

The BRD defines a combined capability shipping in two parts:

  • Customer-Facing Landing Pages — a structured, post-authentication, personalised journey that lets citizens view their case, understand their enforcement stage, and complete the primary action (most often a payment).
  • Customer App — Landing Page Settings module — an internal, governed CMS surface that lets authorised business users edit those Landing Pages under a three-step Author → Reviewer → Publisher workflow with a full audit trail.

2.2 UX Objectives

Predictability

Internal users follow the same Edit → Save → Approve → Publish rhythm on every tab.

WYSIWYE

The editor visually mirrors the live Landing Page so authors predict the customer view.

Progressive Disclosure

Secondary actions ride behind clearly labelled controls keeping the primary path clean.

Accessible by Default

All content tokens, variables, alerts, and modals meet WCAG 2.2 AA.
