Executive SummaryThis document defines the business requirements for the Enforcement Agent Master Data Platform, which aims to consolidate and centralise Enforcement Agent (EA) information into a single master system. It establishes a Single Source of Truth (SSOT), drives data integration and governance, ensures completeness and accuracy of EA profiles, and aligns processes and reporting with the master data to improve reliability and compliance.2. Business Context & BackgroundCurrent EA information is fragmented across multiple legacy systems (optimise, Columbus, Advance, Asset.), causing duplicate entries, inconsistencies, manual reconciliation, process inefficiencies, and risk exposure. A unified EA data platform will remove these silos, standardise attributes, simplify agent management across enforcement processes, IT , payroll, and reporting.3. Business ObjectivesThe platform shall achieve the following objectives:Establish a Single Source of Truth (SSOT) for Enforcement Agent Data: Create a single, authoritative Master EA Database that eliminates fragmented, inconsistent, and duplicated EA information across all operational and legacy systems. 100% of downstream systems must consume EA data exclusively from the Master Record.Ensure Seamless, Real-Time Integration and Sync Transparency Across All Systems: Design and implement a resilient, uni-directional integration framework that ensures updates propagate reliably to all dependent systems in real-time or within defined SLAs. A visible sync health dashboard shall be provided at the EA record level.Implement Robust Data Governance, Compliance, and Security Controls: Enforce role-based access control (RBAC), dual-approval workflow (Keyer/Verifier) for sensitive changes, a comprehensive audit trail for all actions, and automated retention, archival, and leaver-handling processes.Maintain a Complete, Standardised, 360° Enforcement Agent Profile: Capture every critical attribute required for operational processes. Every active EA must have a complete, validated, and up-to-date profile.Align Operational Processes, Workflows, and Reporting with the Master EA Data Model: Modernise and harmonise business processes across Enforcement Operations, HR, Compliance, Payroll, and field management to rely solely on the Master EA Record.4. ScopeIn ScopeCentralised EA system design and implementationIntegration with all consuming systems via API and real-time syncData Governance, Security, and Compliance featuresComplete EA profile data model and mandatory fields enforcementLeaver handling, data retention and archival processesOut of ScopeChanges to non-EA data domains (e.g. Debtor data, Case data)Enhancements outside EA integration and governanceExisting Advance Data migration to Master EA 5. As-Is ProcessThe EA onboarding process begins when the Advance system receives EA onboarding details from the Talos system, such as name, contact information, and address. If the data is successfully received, the EA profile is automatically populated in Advance. If the details are not received, the Advance user manually creates the EA profile.Once the EA profile is available through either method, the entity setup is completed by adding certificate, contract, and bond details. After the setup is completed, reports are automatically generated (daily, weekly, or monthly) and shared with relevant teams such as Finance and Fleet./7. Business Flow (To-Be)In the To-be state, EA onboarding is initiated in the Advance platform where the keyer creates or updates EA details including IT setup and enforcement information. The verifier then reviews and approves the data. Upon approval, the Master EA Platform acting as the single source of truth automatically syncs the updated data to three integrated systems: Asset Register (MAS), Optimise, and Columbus, eliminating manual handoffs./8. High-Level Business RequirementsIDREQUIREMENTBR-01The system shall consolidate all EA records into a single Master EA Database, eliminating fragmented data across Optimise, Columbus, Advance, MHL Portal (Asset Register).BR-02The system shall enforce mandatory field completion and validation rules for every EA record (personal identifiers, contract status, certification, asset assignments).BR-03Any EA record created, updated, or deactivated in the Master EA Database shall be automatically propagated to all consuming downstream systems in near real-time or within defined SLAs.BR-04The system shall provide a unified, read-only 360° EA Profile View for inquiry and operational users without edit permissions.BR-05All sensitive EA data updates shall follow a dual-control (Keyer/Verifier) four-eyes approval workflow before changes take effect.BR-06The system shall enforce Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across all functions, data elements, and field-level visibility.BR-07A full audit trail shall be maintained for all create/update actions including timestamp, actor, source/target system, and result.BR-08The system shall manage EA postcode operating areas with PAF validation; classifications: Primary, Secondary, Potential.BR-09The system shall manage IT asset to EAs with dual-control governance and full audit trails.BR-10The system shall provide per-EA integration status views showing sync success/failure per connected system with detailed audit logs.BR-11Data retention, archival, and leaver-handling processes shall be implemented and enforced per compliance requirements. 9. Assumptions, Constraints & Dependencies10.1 AssumptionsRBAC roles and their mapping to business functions will be defined and agreed with stakeholders before build commences.The Postal Address File (PAF) is accessible for postcode validation within the platform. 10.2 ConstraintsThe Master EA Platform must not negatively impact the performance of downstream consuming systems during data synchronization.All data processing and storage must comply with applicable data protection regulations (e.g. GDPR, UK DPA 2018). 10.3 DependenciesAvailability and readiness of Optimise, Columbus, and Advance API interfaces for integration testing.Completion of data discovery and mapping exercise for legacy EA records prior to migration.Access to PAF service for postcode validation integration. 11.
