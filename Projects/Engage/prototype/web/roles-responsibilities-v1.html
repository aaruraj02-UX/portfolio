Roles & Responsibilities

Role Overview

admin_panel_settings
System Administrator
Admin

Global visibility across all business areas with full configuration access. Manages system settings, SLA thresholds, and oversees task operations organisation-wide.

Key Responsibilities
  • check_circleManage system configuration and SLA settings
  • check_circleOversee all tasks across every business area
  • check_circleConfigure SLA thresholds per priority level
  • check_circleReview audit trails for compliance
  • check_circleHandle escalations beyond manager level
  • check_circleGenerate trend analysis and compliance reports
supervisor_account
Business Area Manager
Manager

Monitors tasks within their business area to ensure SLA compliance. Reassigns workloads, escalates blocked tasks, and maintains operational oversight.

Key Responsibilities
  • check_circleMonitor tasks and SLA proximity within business area
  • check_circleReassign tasks when agents are unavailable
  • check_circleEscalate tasks to senior agents or specialists
  • check_circleCreate ad-hoc tasks and assign to agents
  • check_circleReview completion rates and rejection reasons
  • check_circleEnsure SLA compliance targets are met
person
Field Agent / Assignee
Assigned User / Task Owner

Responsible for performing actions on assigned tasks and completing them within SLA. Updates task status, adds mandatory comments, and manages their task queue.

Key Responsibilities
  • check_circleComplete assigned tasks within SLA deadlines
  • check_circleUpdate task status through predefined actions
  • check_circleAdd mandatory comments documenting actions
  • check_circleAcknowledge critical task notifications
  • check_circlePrioritise queue based on SLA urgency
  • check_circleProvide evidence and comments for auditing
