ProServe Master EA Platform

Open Prototype
  1. Project Overview: Enterprise platform to centralize enforcement agent records into a governed master system.
  2. Business Context: Four disconnected legacy systems created duplicate records, reconciliation overhead, and audit gaps.
  3. Customer Problem: Contract and enforcement teams could not trust profile completeness, sync status, or ownership of critical updates.
  4. UX Strategy: Design for role-based clarity, dual-approval compliance, and transparent integration health without cognitive overload.
  5. Leadership Responsibilities: Led UX framing for RBAC-heavy workflows, approval-state design, and operational adoption strategy.
  6. Stakeholder Collaboration: Worked across contract services, enforcement leadership, IT/security, audit, and support functions.
  7. Research Methodology: Persona modeling, empathy mapping, role-task analysis, and workflow decomposition.
  8. Key Insights: Users needed section-level control, visible status ownership, and confidence in sync outcomes per EA record.
  9. Experience Vision: One platform, one record, one truth with governed edits and full traceability.
  10. Design Process: Mapped As-Is and To-Be flows, then translated BR requirements into task-oriented interaction architecture.
  11. Wireframes & Prototypes: Multi-step profile workflows and approval-state interactions were validated through working HTML screens.
  12. Validation & Testing: UX pack included heuristic/accessibility direction and role-specific review checkpoints.
  13. Challenges & Decision Making: Balanced deep field-level permissions with simplicity via progressive disclosure and visual guardrails.
  14. Business Impact Metrics: Targeted 0 duplicate records, >=99.5% sync success, and 100% sensitive-change four-eyes compliance.
  15. Outcomes: Established a scalable governance model for master-data reliability and reduced exception risk.
  16. UX Leadership Learnings: In regulated systems, UX must operationalize policy as product behavior, not documentation.
  17. Future Opportunities: Add predictive data-quality alerts and proactive downstream sync anomaly insights.

Customer App - Landing Page & Settings Module

Open Prototype
  1. Project Overview: Customer-facing landing journey paired with an internal governed content management module.
  2. Business Context: High-friction assisted channels and inconsistent touchpoints reduced digital resolution effectiveness.
  3. Customer Problem: Citizens needed clearer stage understanding and next-step confidence under enforcement stress.
  4. UX Strategy: Deliver dual-track UX: simple customer journey plus Author-Reviewer-Publisher governance model.
  5. Leadership Responsibilities: Directed structure for role segregation, status lifecycle, and controlled publishing operations.
  6. Stakeholder Collaboration: Aligned compliance, business operations, content owners, product, and engineering.
  7. Research Methodology: Persona-led journey mapping, workflow analysis, and risk-oriented content governance review.
  8. Key Insights: What-you-see-is-what-you-edit parity and immutable variable integrity were critical adoption drivers.
  9. Experience Vision: Increase self-service confidence while giving internal teams safe, auditable content agility.
  10. Design Process: Converted BRD objectives into tabbed template architecture, inline editing, and stateful review workflows.
  11. Wireframes & Prototypes: Interactive prototype validated authoring, approval, refusal, audit, and preview behaviors.
  12. Validation & Testing: Defined acceptance mapping for IA/journeys with WCAG 2.2 AA and no dead-end states.
  13. Challenges & Decision Making: Balanced editing flexibility against legal consistency and runtime personalization safety.
  14. Business Impact Metrics: Targeted improved pay-now conversion, lower content-change lead time, and complete publishing traceability.
  15. Outcomes: Established a governed digital-content operating model with reduced dependency on engineering releases.
  16. UX Leadership Learnings: Governance improves speed when constraints are visible, actionable, and role-aware.
  17. Future Opportunities: Add controlled experimentation on CTA and support-intent journeys with analytics feedback loops.

FieldSync Field App - Task Management & SLA Workflow

Open Prototype
  1. Project Overview: SLA-driven task lifecycle solution for field and web users across business areas.
  2. Business Context: Unstructured task handling created delays, weak accountability, and inconsistent escalation behavior.
  3. Customer Problem: Agents lacked clear next actions while managers lacked immediate visibility of SLA risk.
  4. UX Strategy: Design for operational speed with mandatory accountability and transparent urgency signals.
  5. Leadership Responsibilities: Led requirements consolidation and translated MOM/interview findings into delivery-ready UX artifacts.
  6. Stakeholder Collaboration: Coordinated operations, manager/admin users, delivery teams, and field-process owners.
  7. Research Methodology: Interview synthesis, task analysis, workflow mapping, and heuristic review.
  8. Key Insights: Linear list patterns outperformed grid layouts for field users under time pressure.
  9. Experience Vision: Every assignee knows the next action, every manager sees risk, every action is traceable.
  10. Design Process: Defined status model, action taxonomy, SLA states, and role-based dashboards with filtering.
  11. Wireframes & Prototypes: Built task list/detail, reassignment/escalation flows, and history visibility in working prototypes.
  12. Validation & Testing: Focused on mandatory-comment compliance, SLA readability, and action clarity under workload.
  13. Challenges & Decision Making: Reduced friction from validation rules using inline guidance and strong state communication.
  14. Business Impact Metrics: Targeted faster at-risk task identification, fewer SLA breaches, and better delegation traceability.
  15. Outcomes: Delivered a structured operational model for scalable task governance and performance monitoring.
  16. UX Leadership Learnings: Operational UX succeeds when speed and compliance are designed as one system.
  17. Future Opportunities: Add workload-aware assignment recommendations and adaptive notification tuning.

FinOps Admin Dashboard KPI Metrics

Open Prototype

  2. Business Context: Oversight was fragmented across multiple sources, delaying decision-making and escalation.
  3. Customer Problem: Admin and operations leaders lacked a single, trustworthy view of system and workflow health.
  4. UX Strategy: Build an exception-first analytics experience with actionable drill-down paths.
  5. Leadership Responsibilities: Defined KPI hierarchy, information density strategy, and role-safe visibility model.
  6. Stakeholder Collaboration: Worked with finance, automation, operations managers, and engineering to align KPI definitions.
  7. Research Methodology: Persona and task-flow analysis with focus on bottleneck detection and reporting workflows.
  8. Key Insights: Decision-makers need immediate trend direction and shortest path from metric to underlying records.
  9. Experience Vision: Replace fragmented reporting with one dashboard that supports rapid, evidence-based intervention.
  10. Design Process: Structured KPI cards, filter model, trend states, and insights panels for progressive disclosure.
  11. Wireframes & Prototypes: Dashboard compositions and monitoring patterns were explored through staged prototype assets.
  12. Validation & Testing: Evaluated readability, threshold comprehension, and filter responsiveness expectations.
  13. Challenges & Decision Making: Balanced rich data density with cognitive load and API performance constraints.
  14. Business Impact Metrics: Targeted replacement of 3+ separate views and bottleneck detection time reduced from hours to seconds.
  15. Outcomes: Established executive-level observability baseline and clearer operational governance signals.
  16. UX Leadership Learnings: Dashboards create value only when they trigger fast, accountable actions.
  17. Future Opportunities: Add predictive anomalies and role-specific thresholds with configurable alerts.

DocProcess Stage 3 & Stage 5 Response Generation

Open Prototype
  1. Project Overview: AI-assisted response generation for Stage 3 and Stage 5 correspondence in ANPS workflows.
  2. Business Context: Manual letter assembly across systems caused inconsistency, low throughput, and quality variance.
  3. Customer Problem: Processing agents needed faster completion with confidence; QA needed precise edit transparency.
  4. UX Strategy: Use explainable AI outcomes (Green/Amber/Red) with structured human review and routing controls.
  5. Leadership Responsibilities: Led trust model, queue behavior, QA diff review design, and governance-ready flow alignment.
  6. Stakeholder Collaboration: Worked with processing teams, QA leads, workflow managers, and knowledge owners.
  7. Research Methodology: Requirement traceability from PBDs, role analysis, and workflow scenario mapping.
  8. Key Insights: Adoption depends on visible AI rationale, explicit exception handling, and reliable audit defensibility.
  9. Experience Vision: Shift from manual composition to assisted review where humans retain control and accountability.
  10. Design Process: Designed letter-generation states, review actions, QA loops, and knowledge-source dependency surfaces.
  11. Wireframes & Prototypes: Working screens covered correspondence authoring, mitigation exceptions, queueing, and QA review.
  12. Validation & Testing: Focused on diff clarity, routing correctness, confidence communication, and keyboard accessibility.
  13. Challenges & Decision Making: Balanced AI speed gains with policy integrity, portal dependencies, and privacy controls.
  14. Business Impact Metrics: Targeted reduced handling time, QA accuracy uplift, 24-hour knowledge updates, and full event auditability.
  15. Outcomes: Established a scalable AI-assisted operational model with explicit quality-control safeguards.
  16. UX Leadership Learnings: Effective AI UX leadership is governance leadership expressed through product behavior.
  17. Future Opportunities: Introduce confidence calibration dashboards and automated knowledge-drift detection.
