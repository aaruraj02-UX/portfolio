How should the current step be indicated in the workflow?DiscussionThe current step should only be visually highlighted.AnswerCurrent stage is highlighted.Steps are not selectable/clickable.Users cannot jump between stages.UX Decision✅ Use a non-clickable step indicator showing current progress only.Q2.
