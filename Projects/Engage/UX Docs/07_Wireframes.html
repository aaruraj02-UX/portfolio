Annotation WF-07: Toasts use role="status" (non-urgent, polite) or role="alert" (urgent, assertive) per WCAG 4.1.3. SLA approaching and overdue toasts use role="alert" to ensure screen reader interruption. All toasts include action links for immediate navigation. Background colours use error/warning tokens — text always white with =4.5:1 contrast. Toasts auto-dismiss after 8 seconds (non-urgent) or persist until dismissed (urgent). Dismiss button always available (not shown for brevity). Maximum one toast at a time — queued if multiple triggered in rapid succession.
